Minimum number of links: 2, 2, 3, 3, 3, 3 for n = 2..7.  The draft's guess
1 + floor(n/2) (2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4) fails at n = 6 and n = 7.  All 38 (n = 6) and
44 (n = 7) three-link solutions were re-checked with the exhaustive router, and
the only 5 (n = 6) and 6 (n = 7) two-link induced covers are all non-compact.

Maximum number of links: floor(n^2/2) (A007590): dominoes plus, for odd n, one
straight 3-cell link always form a compact solution, and more links would need
more than n^2 cells.  The draft says ceiling(n^2/2) (A000982), which is wrong for
odd n (5 links cannot fit in 9 cells).
